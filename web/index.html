Expand Your Knowledge and Improve Your Community

Offie is an offline library that gives you the hands-on skills to build a self-sufficient community that can work together for the public good. This system is built for portability and to avoid subscriptions, hidden data collection, restrictions, and full reliance on the internet.

Explore Topics → Choose Drive…

Build Self-Sufficiency

Build the basic skills to look after yourself and provide service to your community. Learn how to gather food and water, build shelter, render first aid, and more.

Build Infrastructure

Fix systems and build your own infrastructure. Search tech repair guides, electrical connections, electronics, and communication networks.

Learn From the Real World

Combine your online research and real world findings into a personalized continuous learning library built from your own experiences in nature, abroad, and in unfamiliar territory.

What happens to the drive

We add one folder, OfflineLibrary. Nothing else on the drive is changed or deleted.

Best format: exFAT. It works on Mac, Windows and Linux and holds big files.

Four short parts, top to bottom. You can leave and come back: downloads keep going, and you can build again at any time.

Which computers should open this drive?

We put a small reader app on the drive for each one you tick (about 10 MB each). Ticking them all is safest: you may not know what computer you'll find.

Phones and tablets don't need this. Install the free Kiwix app, then open the files in the drive's zim folder.

Test it on this computer

Opens the library from the drive, the same way it will work with no internet.

Using the drive with no internet

1 · Unplug safely

Stop the reader, then eject the drive before pulling it out.

2 · Plug into any computer

Mac, Windows or Linux. No internet or install needed.

3 · Double-click START

Open the OfflineLibrary folder and pick yours:

START-Mac.command START-Windows.bat START-Linux.sh

4 · Read in the browser

The home page opens with a link for every topic and library on the drive.

A README-FIRST.txt file with these same steps is saved on the drive too.
